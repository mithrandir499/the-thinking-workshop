STM32 Black Pill:
From a Blinking LED to Reading Silicon

My first hands-on exploration of the STM32 Black Pill—connecting an ST-LINK V2 programmer, configuring GPIO using STM32CubeMX, writing firmware in C, flashing the microcontroller, setting hardware breakpoints, and observing the GPIOC output register change in real time.

03 / About

A workshop, not a showroom.

I don't want this place to pretend that everything is finished.

The Thinking Workshop is a personal space for following curiosity. Some things here may become essays. Some may become circuits. Some may simply remain questions.

The common thread is simple: to look closely, learn something, make something, and occasionally change one's mind.
